How it works
Photo – load a picture.
Depth map – the AI makes a grey picture where white means near and black means far. It runs on your computer; the first time, it downloads about 100 MB.
Camera move – near things slide more than far things, which makes it feel 3D (that's called parallax).
Export – saves an MP4 video to your Downloads folder.
Everything else (depth fine-tuning, keyframes, lens, colour, file settings) lives in the other tabs.

AI files (one time only)
Some browsers, such as Edge on iPhone and iPad with a page opened from Files, block the app from downloading these itself. Ordinary downloads still work, so:
1. Tap each link and save the file (it goes to Downloads in the Files app):
